# Skills Taxonomy

This is the only definition of "skill" the engine is allowed to use. No concept may claim
a skill that isn't listed here, and no skill may be claimed without pointing to the specific
moment in the concept where a Riser actually does it.

A claim of the form "this builds critical thinking" is not valid on its own. A valid claim
names the sub-skill and the moment: "the moment where the Riser must reject the
thinnest-ring year as the fire year and use scar data instead — that is Confound-checking,
and the concept does not work without it."

---

## Research
- **Sourcing** — judging which available evidence actually bears on the question, vs. present but irrelevant
- **Cross-referencing** — checking one source against another, knowing what to do when they disagree
- **Distinguishing claim from evidence** — noticing when something is asserted vs. actually demonstrated

## Critical thinking
- **Confound-checking** — generating an alternative explanation for a pattern before accepting the first plausible one
- **Assumption-surfacing** — naming what would have to be true for a conclusion to hold, and checking if it is
- **Resisting the obvious-wrong-answer** — noticing when the easiest read of evidence is incomplete or false

## Analysis
- **Pattern extraction** — finding a trend or structure in data that isn't stated outright
- **Quantitative reasoning** — using numbers to compare, rate, or predict, not just report
- **Decomposition** — breaking a complex claim into the smaller sub-claims it depends on

## Mind-mapping / structuring thought
- **Relationship-mapping** — representing how several factors connect or influence each other, not just listing them
- **Prioritization** — deciding which of many true things matters most for the question at hand

## Evidence-producing
- **Making a claim demonstrable** — generating or capturing evidence that would let someone else verify a claim
- **Recording rigor** — capturing evidence precisely enough to be checked later, not impressionistically

## Experimenting
- **Hypothesis-forming** — stating a specific, falsifiable expectation before testing it
- **Variable isolation** — recognizing what must be held constant to test one thing at a time
- **Predict-then-check** — committing to a prediction before seeing the result, then reconciling any mismatch

## Building / prototyping
- **Translating idea into concrete form** — going from abstract understanding to something made, tested, or manipulated by hand
- **Iterating on failure** — using a failed first attempt as information, not discarding it

## Debating
- **Constructing a position from evidence** — arguing a specific claim using specific support
- **Steelmanning the other side** — accurately representing a view you disagree with before countering it
- **Updating under good counter-evidence** — changing a position when given genuinely strong reason to

## Philosophical thinking
- **Questioning a premise others take for granted** — noticing an unstated value judgment and naming it
- **Reasoning about "ought" not just "is"** — moving from what's true to what should be done, and defending that move

## Cultural thinking
- **Perspective-taking across difference** — reasoning how the same situation looks different from another vantage point, era, or value set
- **Recognizing context-dependence** — understanding a rule/norm/explanation that holds in one setting may not hold in another, and why

## Writing
- **Argument sequencing** — laying out reasoning in an order that builds a case, not a list of thoughts
- **Precision over padding** — saying exactly what's meant without filler

## Creativity
- **Generating genuinely distinct options** — producing more than one real solution, not one solution restated
- **Recombination** — applying a concept or method from one domain to solve a problem in another

---

## Rule for the engine
Every generated concept must state:
1. Which sub-skill(s) above it exercises (by exact name from this list)
2. The specific moment in the concept where the Riser does that thing
3. Why the concept doesn't work if that moment is removed

If a concept can't produce this for a claimed skill, the skill claim is dropped, not kept as
aspirational framing.

## Change process
This is a living document. Edit it directly to add, remove, or redefine skills/sub-skills.
The engine reads this file at generation time — no code change is required for a skill-list
update to take effect.
